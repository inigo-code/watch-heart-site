Privacy

Your diary is yours. Watch Heart has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares what you log.

Effective 28 October 2026

Without an account

Everything you log stays on your phone: your diary, reviews, ratings, watchlist, imports and settings. None of it is sent to me. If you uninstall the app, it’s gone, unless you made a backup file.

What the app asks other services

To find titles and posters, Watch Heart sends what you search for, and the ids of titles you log, to these services. They see your IP address the way any website would, but not your diary.

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) for films, series, anime and posters. TMDB’s privacy policy
  • TVmaze for series and what’s trending. TVmaze’s privacy policy
  • Expo, which delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, Expo learns the app version and that it runs on Android. Expo’s privacy policy

With an account

An account is optional. It backs up your diary and keeps your devices in sync. If you create one, these are stored with Supabase, the service that hosts Watch Heart’s accounts:

  • Your email address and your password, which is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Your diary entries and watchlist: titles, dates, ratings, reviews, tags and notes.

Each account can read only its own diary; the database refuses everyone else. I don’t read your diary. Your email is used only to sign you in, confirm your account and reset your password.

On your phone only

  • Fingerprint lock. Android checks your fingerprint or screen lock. The app only learns whether it matched.
  • Imports. Files from Letterboxd, IMDb and other apps are read on your phone. What you import joins your diary like anything else you log.
  • Stories and photos. Story images are made on your phone. They go only where you share or save them. The app asks for access to your photos only to save a story there.
  • Your TMDB key, if you add one in Settings, stays on your phone.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings, tap your email address under Account, then Delete account. Your account and everything synced to it are deleted at once and for good. You can also ask by email; see Delete your account. To remove what’s on your phone, uninstall the app.

Children

Watch Heart is not made for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly keep accounts for them. If you think a child has made one, write to me and I’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date. If the change is significant, the app will say so.
