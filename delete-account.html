Delete your account

You can delete your Watch Heart account, and everything synced to it, whenever you like.

In the app

  1. Open Watch Heart and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap your email address.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account.

It happens at once and can’t be undone.

By email

If you can’t open the app, write from the email address you signed up with to inigo@tosstheworld.org with the subject “Delete my Watch Heart account”. I’ll delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply when it’s done.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and password.
  • Every diary entry and watchlist item synced to your account: titles, dates, ratings, reviews, tags and notes.
  • Your profile: name, bio, photo, favourites and yearly goal.

Nothing is kept afterwards.

What stays

The diary on your phone stays there, so you can keep using Watch Heart without an account. To remove it too, uninstall the app. Story images you shared or saved stay wherever you put them.

Before you go

To keep a copy, export it first. In Settings, under Your data: Export backup, Export as spreadsheet, or Export films for Letterboxd.
