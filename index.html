Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely: no ads, no subscriptions and no paid extras.

Where is my diary kept?

On your phone. If you sign in, a copy is kept in your private account so your devices stay in sync; nobody else can read it.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. An account only adds backup and sync.

Where do the titles and posters come from?

From TMDB, the free film and TV database, from AniList for anime, and from TVmaze for series.

How do I post a story to Instagram?

Open Recap, choose the month and a look, tap Share to story, then pick Instagram and Story. You can save it to your photos too.

How do updates arrive?

Smaller improvements arrive inside the app by themselves. For new versions, F-Droid updates it like any other app; with the APK, download the new one over the old and your diary stays.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, tap your email under Account, then Delete account. There’s also a page for it.

Is it on Google Play or iPhone?

It’s an Android app. Install the APK here, or get it with F-Droid. Google Play is next.
